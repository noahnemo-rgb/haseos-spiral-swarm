# Bound by the charter

This repository is the workshop. HAOS and DSM are running systems here. They are bound by HASEOS-IDAO, not by a second constitution written in this repo.

Law-as-files lives at https://github.com/noahnemo-rgb/HASEOS-IDAO — Article VII and Appendix 06. This page restates those bindings only. It is not new law.

## Bindings (restated, not enacted here)

- Ethics First. Fail closed when a gate, cert, or HITL token is missing.
- Memory that lasts is inspectable. HITL authorizes durable write.
- A guest remains a guest. Fusion is false.
- Authority passed on can only shrink. Revoke kills the chain.
- DSM may freeze, refuse, park, or revoke standing. It may not invent history, silently drop history, or punish outside declared scope.
- HITL is final human authority over promotion, unfreeze, and charter amendment until a ratified procedure replaces that sentence.
