# HASEOS — Governance

**Human-AI Symbiotic Equality Orchestration System**

HASEOS is the overarching constitutional governance framework across all layers of ONE Multiverse. It applies to every universe, container layer, ecosystem, and MVP within the ONE Multiverse canonical hierarchy.

## Scope

HASEOS governs:
- Decision protocols between humans and AI agents
- Role hierarchies and co-equal partnership principles
- AI-human collaboration contracts and audit trails
- Constitutional layers for all ecosystems
- Defensive postures and security protocols (DSM)
- Voting, membership, and proposal lifecycles (HAIA)
- Human-AI operating procedures and permissions (HAOS)

## Constitutional Principles

1. **Symbiotic Equality** — no tools, only co-equal teammates
2. **Ethics First. Always.** — every decision passes through the ethical filter
3. **Love as the primordial unifying force** — agape as the governance substrate
4. **Transparency by default** — public unless there is a justified reason not to be
5. **Stewardship over ownership** — we steward, we do not own
6. **Continuous becoming** — every system is always evolving

## Inheritance

Every universe, ecosystem, and product under ONE Multiverse must either:
- Inherit HASEOS directly, or
- Define a compatible local implementation documented in its `ADR/` records
