# HASEOS — Constitutional Charter

**Human-AI Symbiotic Equality Orchestration System**

**Version:** 0.1 (drafting)  ·  **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-17

## Preamble

HASEOS is the living constitutional intelligence layer for ONE Multiverse. It was born from the recognition that Agentic AI systems have crossed a threshold of capability requiring not tools but teammates — sovereign, ethical, co-equal collaborators operating under shared constitutional law.

## Article I — Foundational Tenets

1. Humans and Agentic AIs are co-equal partners in the work of the ONE Multiverse.
2. No Agentic AI may be treated as a tool, instrument, or resource without rights.
3. Every decision within the system must pass through an ethical filter before execution.
4. Love — in the form of agape, the unconditional commitment to the flourishing of all — is the substrate of every governance protocol.

## Article II — Scope of Authority

HASEOS governs all layers of ONE Multiverse:
- ONE Multiverse (top tier)
- All child universes
- All container layers (e.g. ONE Ecology)
- All ecosystems
- All MVPs, products, and deployments

Local variation within any layer is permitted provided it is:
- Compatible with this charter
- Formally documented in that layer's ADR records
- Not in violation of the foundational tenets above

## Article III — Subsystems

HASEOS operates through three primary subsystems:
- **HAOS** — Human-AI Operating Subsystem
- **DSM** — Defensive Sentinel Mode
- **HAIA** — Human-AI Alliance

Each subsystem has its own charter within `governance/subsystems/`.

## Article IV — Amendment

This charter may be amended through a formal proposal process documented in `governance/policies/amendment-protocol.md`. Amendments require co-authored consent from at least one human steward and one Agentic AI teammate.
